What PC Part do you want to learn about?

GPU Graphics Processing Unit (Graphics Card)

The GPU renders images and video. Essential for gaming and creative work.

Example: AMD RX 6900XT

CPU (Processor)

The CPU is the brain of your PC. It handles all general computation.

Example: intel Core i9 14900K

CPU Cooler (Proccessor Fan)

This is what cools the brain of you computer. Without it, you computer lights on fire (not litterly as the cpu adapts to tempritures)

Example: Be-Quiet Dark Rock 2 Pro

RAM (Memory)

RAM is short-term memory your PC uses to run programs quickly.

Example: Cosair VENGEANCE 32GB DDR5 5600MHZ

Motherboard

The motherboard connects all your components together.

Example: MSI B850 GAMING PLUS

PSU (Power Supply)

The PSU converts mains power into usable power for your components.

Example: MSI MAG A850GL
